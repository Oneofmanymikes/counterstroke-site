# The Counterstroke

A cleaned-up and re-typeset edition of Kenneth Macksey's narrative wargame *The Counterstroke* (Canadian Army, B-GL-309-007/FT-001, c.1990), with the book's seven maps redrawn as an interactive page.

- `index.html`: the landing page
- `book.html`: the full text with its photographs, maps and diagrams
- `maps.html`: the redrawn maps
- the PDF of the clean edition

## Credits

- **Author:** Kenneth Macksey, Major (Retd), MC, a former officer in the Royal Tank Regiment and a military writer, who also wrote *First Clash*.
- **Publisher:** Department of National Defence, Canada, publication B-GL-309-007/FT-001, issued on the authority of the Chief of Defence Staff (comments to FMC Headquarters, SO2 Doctrine Coordination). No date of publication is printed; the story is set in 1990.
- **Photographs and maps:** reproduced from the original, which does not credit individual photographers. The original maps are drawn on the 1:100,000 sheet Schwandorf (C 6738, Series M648).
- **Source:** the scan the Canadian Army posted on canada.ca in 2025 (https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/dnd-mdn/army/lineofsight/articleimages/2025/01/Counterstroke-B-GL-309-007FT-001-OCR-lr.pdf), made from the copy in the Land Force Command Headquarters Main Library.
- **This edition:** the re-typesetting, OCR corrections, cleaned images, abbreviation expansions, redrawn maps and stage summaries are new work.
